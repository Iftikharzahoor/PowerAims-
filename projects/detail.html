Dams & Hydropower

5th Periodic Inspection – Khanpur Dam

CLIENT / PARTNER Water & Power Development Authority (WAPDA)
LOCATION Haripur, KPK, Pakistan
STATUS Completed Successfully

Detailed Scope of Services

The scope of periodic inspection included a comprehensive review and on-site assessment of the dam structure, foundations, abutments, and reservoir rim to evaluate stability, seepage, settlement, erosion, and other signs of distress. It also covered inspection of spillways, outlet works, gates, valves, energy dissipation arrangements, and associated mechanical and electrical systems to verify operational reliability.

The inspection further assessed hydrological and hydraulic adequacy by reviewing design flood standards against updated hydrological data. Based on the findings, Consultants identified deficiencies, prioritized risks, and provided recommendations for remedial measures, maintenance, or rehabilitation to ensure dam safety and compliance with regulatory requirements.

Key Engineering Highlights & Deliverables

  • Dam Safety Surveillance & Risk Evaluation
  • Spillway & Hydro-Mechanical Gates Inspection
  • Hydrological Flood Frequency Re-evaluation
  • Foundation Seepage & Stability Analysis
